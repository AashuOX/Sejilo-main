SejiloChat is a social app for Android and Windows. When you have a connection it behaves like any other messenger: a feed, profiles, stories, direct messages. When you do not, it keeps delivering — by handing messages from phone to phone over Bluetooth Low Energy until one of them reaches the recipient or reaches the internet.

Get the app How the offline part works

Two ways to deliver one message

Every message is handed to whichever transport can carry it, and the app tells you which one did. There is no separate “offline mode” to remember to switch on.

Online

Signed in and connected: messages, posts, stories and profiles go through the API and are stored server-side, so a second device sees the same history.

Bluetooth, direct

No internet, recipient in range: the two devices establish an encrypted session and the message is handed over directly. Roughly 10–30 m indoors.

Bluetooth, relayed

No internet, recipient out of range: nearby devices running SejiloChat pass the message along, each hop covering its own 10–30 m, up to a fixed hop limit.

Synchronised

Whichever device on that path gets a connection first uploads the queue, so messages sent while everyone was offline land in normal history afterwards.

What it is honest about

Bluetooth range is not unlimited and cannot be made unlimited. A relayed message needs a chain of people who are also running the app; with nobody in between, there is no path, and the message waits in the outbox until there is one. Mesh delivery is best-effort — good for a classroom, a festival, a building, a power cut. It is not a substitute for a mobile network.

Messages are signed, and relays cannot read or edit them

A relay is a stranger’s phone, so the protocol is built to assume it is hostile. Each device holds an Ed25519 key pair; every frame that leaves it is signed, and any hop that alters a byte invalidates the signature and is dropped. Conversation content is encrypted for the recipient with a key derived from an X25519 exchange, so a relay forwards a payload it cannot open.
